Privacy Policy

Data Collection

Breakdown collects a player-chosen display name and an anonymous device identifier (Apple's identifierForVendor) solely to power the global leaderboard. This data is stored on secure servers and is never linked to your real identity. No accounts, no email addresses, no sign-ins.

Leaderboard

Your display name and device identifier are submitted to our leaderboard when you complete a level. You can delete your leaderboard data at any time from within the app. Display names are chosen by you and should not contain personal information.

Ads

Breakdown includes optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one (e.g. to access the leaderboard). AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's privacy policy. You can remove ads entirely with the one-time premium upgrade inside the app.

Data Sharing & Sale

No personal data is sold or shared with third parties for advertising or any commercial purpose. Leaderboard data (display name and anonymous device ID) is visible to other players on the leaderboard only.

In-App Purchases

Any in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or handle your payment information. Purchase transactions are subject to Apple's privacy policy.
